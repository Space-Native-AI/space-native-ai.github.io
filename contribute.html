What happens next

Cycle I is a research-community building process.

  1. Synthesis. Contributions are grouped into emerging research themes.
  2. Feedback. Selected submissions receive concise editorial feedback and may be invited into public discussion.
  3. Community input. Questions and themes may be opened for comments from other participants.
  4. Featured Contributions. A small number of especially generative questions or concepts will be highlighted in the Cycle I Review.
  5. Follow-up. Where useful, we will connect contributors working on related problems and use the strongest themes to shape future community activities.

This is community recognition and editorial selection, not peer review, publication acceptance, funding, or a scientific endorsement.

Selection lens

What makes a contribution useful?

We are looking for questions that can generate research, discussion, collaboration, benchmarks, experiments or future projects.

01 / SIGNIFICANCE

Research significance

Does the contribution identify a consequential problem rather than merely rebrand an Earth use case?

02 / SPACE-NATIVE

Space-native necessity

Do physical conditions, mission constraints or scientific opportunities materially change the problem?

03 / AI & LEARNING

Machine-learning relevance

Is there a genuine learning, autonomy, reasoning, adaptation or decision problem?

04 / GENERATIVE VALUE

Potential to lead somewhere

Could the idea become a useful discussion, collaboration, benchmark, experiment or project direction?
