About the initiative

Built around
open scientific questions.

Space-Native AI is an emerging independent initiative connecting machine learning, autonomous scientific discovery and research beyond Earth.

Identity & purpose

Space-first problems. Machine-learning questions.

Our aim is to establish an interdisciplinary conversation about what intelligence needs to learn, observe and decide when scientific activity takes place outside familiar terrestrial conditions.

Space-Native AI starts as an independent, openly accessible research agenda. It is not currently an incorporated association, an accredited academic institution, a conference-approved workshop or an active space mission.

Future talks, research tracks and workshop proposals are aspirations, not confirmed institutional partnerships or accepted conference events.
