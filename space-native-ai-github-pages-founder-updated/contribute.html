Submission guidance

A question worth discussing beats an oversized proposal.

We welcome researchers, engineers, students and interested contributors from around the world.

Define the challenge

What do we need to understand or learn that is not already straightforward in terrestrial settings?

Make the space constraint meaningful

Specify whether the issue involves dynamics, autonomy, communications, computation, power, materials or experimental cost.

Outline evidence

How would the community know whether a future approach succeeded? A metric, simulation or testable hypothesis is enough.

Before posting

Contribute openly. Protect unpublished work.

All GitHub Issues are public and attached to the submitting GitHub account. Do not submit personal details, confidential designs, patent-sensitive material, private research data or third-party copyrighted content without permission.

Contributors retain their rights in their original submissions. A submission does not grant us permission to reproduce it in an external publication or commercial product without further authorization. We may reply and discuss publicly within the issue itself.
