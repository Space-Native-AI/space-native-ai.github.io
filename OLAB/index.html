OLAB / SYSTEM CONCEPT 01

Orbital Laboratory
for Adaptive Biology

A configurable experimental system for bounded, closed-loop biological research in low Earth orbit.

OLAB links calibrated observations, uncertainty-aware state estimation and predefined experimental actions so that an orbital experiment can respond to measured conditions without relying on unconstrained autonomous control.

Stage Ground demonstrator definition Focus Protein R&D in microgravity Base Hessen, Germany
01

Operating constraint

Orbital experiments cannot assume that the next useful measurement is known in advance.

LEO experiments operate with finite samples and reagents, limited intervention opportunities, intermittent communications, strict containment and fixed host resources. If the observed state deviates from expectation, a rigid pre-programmed sequence can consume scarce experimental time without collecting the most informative next evidence.

Finite materialSamples and reagents are mission-limited.
Restricted interventionGround response is not continuously available.
Bounded action spaceOnly validated actions are permitted onboard.
02

Lab in the loop

Closed-loop experimentation, with explicit limits.

The control concept is intentionally conservative: observe the experiment, estimate its state with calibrated references, select only from approved actions, execute, verify and record the result.

01ObserveAcquire selected measurements.
02EstimateRepresent state and uncertainty.
03DecideChoose a permitted next action.
04ActExecute under defined constraints.
05VerifyCheck response against reference criteria.
06RepeatUpdate the experimental state.

Design principle: autonomy is bounded by experiment rules, host interfaces, containment requirements and explicit stop conditions.

03

First application

Protein R&D in microgravity.

The first OLAB configuration is being defined around a protein experiment adapter. The objective is not to claim a generic microgravity advantage, but to establish whether a controlled experiment can make traceable, evidence-based decisions when measurements differ from the planned trajectory.

Potential later adapters may address expression or function studies, crystallisation and formulation. These are development directions, not completed products.

Engineering programme

Development is organised around evidence, interfaces and qualification readiness.

01

Reference & endpoint definition

Define measurable states, acceptance criteria, uncertainty representation and explicit stop conditions.

02

Ground integrated demonstrator

Integrate sensing, decision logic and one experimental adapter in a controlled terrestrial test environment.

03

Independent interface review

Screen containment, power, data, thermal and host-interface assumptions against external requirements.

04

Flight-readiness pathway

Translate validated ground evidence into a later qualification and host-admission programme.

Verification statusGround engineering and validation programme in definition. Flight qualification and orbital deployment have not been completed; later operation requires separate host admission, qualification and funding.
