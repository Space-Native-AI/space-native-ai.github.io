Our premise
Space is not just another destination for AI. It is a new condition for intelligence.
Research agenda

Questions at the frontier of intelligent systems.

Our focus is methodological: what must change in machine learning when physics, experiments and mission resources cannot be taken for granted?

01 / ADAPTATION

Learning under altered physics

Generalization, predictive models and reliable adaptation when space conditions differ from Earth-trained assumptions.

Physical AIWorld ModelsShift & Reliability
02 / DISCOVERY

Autonomous scientific discovery

Closed-loop experiments that select measurements, learn from observations and refine scientific hypotheses with limited intervention.

Active LearningAgentic ScienceExperiment Design
03 / CONSTRAINTS

Intelligence within mission limits

Learning and decision-making under finite energy, computation, communication windows and experimental opportunities.

Budgeted LearningSafe DecisionsInformation Gain
